4G LTE SIM Network

Standalone / Remote Lands / Solar

Inserts any Sri Lankan 4G Data SIM (Dialog, Mobitel, Hutch). Ideal for farm lands, coconut estates, construction sites, or remote houses without Fiber/SLT internet.

  • Zero Wi-Fi Router Required
  • Works with Solar Panel + Battery
  • Direct Phone Alerts via 4G App
  • MicroSD Card + Cloud Storage
